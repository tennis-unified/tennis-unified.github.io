# Tennis Biomechanics: The 1-Inch Punch vs Linear Displacement — Stance Stability & Kinetic Release

Comprehensive biomechanical study examining the difference between linear arm displacement and Bruce Lee's 1-to-2 inch punch principle in modern tennis groundstrokes.

## Key Technical Themes:
1. **The Linear Displacement Fallacy:** Why swinging the arm across 70–100 cm destroys racket head speed through antagonistic co-contraction.
2. **The 1–2 Inch Punch Principle:** At impact ($4 - 5\text{ ms}$), the hand moves only $2.5 - 5\text{ cm}$, releasing kinetic energy generated by the entire body.
3. **Martial Arts Stances (Tấn Pháp & Bộ Pháp):**
   * **Horse Stance / Mã Bộ (Trung Bình Tấn):** Foundation of the modern open and semi-open stance.
   * **Front Stance / Đinh Tấn (Cung Bộ):** Foundation of the closed/neutral step-in drive.
4. **The Somatic Kinetic Transmission Vector (The Red Force Line):** Feet $\to$ Knees $\to$ Hips (Transmission gearbox) $\to$ Torso / Core coil $\to$ Shoulder & Arm $\to$ 1-2 inch release at the strings.
5. **Stability Platform Precondition:** Power cannot be transferred from an unstable base.

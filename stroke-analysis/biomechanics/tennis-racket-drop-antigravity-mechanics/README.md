# Racket Drop & Antigravity Biomechanics: The Extended C-Curve

This directory contains research on the biomechanical phenomenon of the subtle racket drop, unweighting, the "antigravity" rebound, and the continuous Extended C-Curve across forehand, backhand, and volley.

## Core Concepts
1. **Unweighting (Antigravity):** Releasing grip pressure to 2–3/10 allows gravity to accelerate the racket mass into the slot for free ($E_p \rightarrow E_k$).
2. **Extended C-Curve:** Tracing a continuous curved loop rather than pulling straight back eliminates antagonistic muscular resistance and primes the Stretch-Shortening Cycle (SSC).
3. **Forehand, Backhand & Volley Drops:** Three manifestations of stroke priming that produce effortless power, penetration, and lifelong joint protection.

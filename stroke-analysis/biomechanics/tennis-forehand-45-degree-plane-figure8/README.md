## Overview

This analysis covers:
1. **The 45° Geometric Constraint:** Why the hitting arm must operate within a strict 45° arc anterior to the chest to preserve joint integrity and kinetic power.
2. **Horizontal Figure-8 ($\infty$) Dynamics:** Physics of centripetal acceleration ($F_c = \frac{m \cdot v^2}{r}$) eliminating jerky decelerations and boosting racket head speed (RHS).
3. **The 5 Kinematic Phases:** Unit Turn, Gravitational Drop on the 45° plane, Lag & Elastic Snap, Forward Contact (30-40cm out front), and Windshield Wiper Follow-Through.
4. **Visual Blueprints & Diagrams:** Top-down 2D blueprint, 3D vector trajectories, original coaching notes, and vector SVG mapping.
5. **Prescriptive Drills:** The 45° Wall Drill, Mirror Figure-8 Infinity Drills, and 1-2 Cadence Freeze.

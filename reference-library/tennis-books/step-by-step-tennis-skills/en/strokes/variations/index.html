Variations in Stroke Techniques¶

Every player develops their own style within the framework of solid fundamentals. This chapter covers acceptable variations for each stroke.

Groundstroke Adaptations¶

High Balls¶

  • Higher contact - adjust preparation
  • More topspin - brush more aggressively
  • Step back - create space

Low Balls¶

  • Bend knees more - get down
  • Open face - lift over net
  • Shorten swing - control over power

Wide Balls¶

  • Run around (forehand) or stretch (backhand)
  • Open stance - recover faster
  • Slice option - defensive

Short Balls¶

  • Move forward - attack
  • Hit on rise - take time away
  • Approach shot - move to net

Developing Your Style¶

  1. Master fundamentals first - technique before style
  2. Experiment in practice - find what feels natural
  3. Match your physique - tall players: flatter; shorter: more spin
  4. Consider surface - clay: more spin; grass: flatter
  5. Build on strengths - weaponize your best shots

Common Principles Across All Variations¶

Regardless of style, these remain constant: - Balance at contact - Eyes on ball through impact - Complete follow-through - Quick recovery to ready - Kinetic chain sequence

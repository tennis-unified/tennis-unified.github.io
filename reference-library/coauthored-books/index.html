Tennis Unified Handbook — 30-Chapter CORE Biomechanics System¶

The comprehensive unified system covering all aspects of modern tennis:

  • Part I: Foundation (Chapters 1-7) — The problem, tool, precision, outcome, energy store, transmission, CORE system
  • Part II: Sensory & Motor Control (Chapters 8-11) — Quiet Eye, vestibular stability, fascia/proprioception, tensegrity
  • Part III: Kinetic Engine (Chapters 12-15) — Stretch-shortening cycle, figure-8 regulation, 45° swing plane, braking & impulse
  • Part IV: Expression & Footwork (Chapters 16-18) — Jin (unified force), Hu-Thuc footwork, rooting
  • Part V: Stroke Systems (Chapters 19-25) — Forehand push, 1H backhand saber, 2H backhand hybrid, serve, return, volley/overhead, slice/drop/lob
  • Part VI: Tactics & Patterns (Chapters 26-27) — Court geometry, singles patterns & doubles systems
  • Part VII: Complete System (Chapters 28-30) — Mental game, physical preparation, unified quick reference

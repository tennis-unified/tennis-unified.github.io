Analytics

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2020-04-26) · Original Article ↗

Analytics at the top level is very important. For junior development , we are decades away from using it time efficiently and cost effectively. But slowly more and more folks will be incorporating big data analysis at all levels.

The interpreter of the data is key.

Gilbert makes some excellent points about the limitations of statistical analysis and the importance of being consistent for some players.

At present day, this type of analysis is available to the wealthy on Tour.

Long term, as AI surpasses human intelligence, the conclusions from the data will be better than having flawed human minds making poor assumptions from the data.

Also, nobody ever questions the data collection itself which is done by humans and can be flawed.

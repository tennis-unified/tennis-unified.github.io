Opelka’s Unique Pinpoint

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2020-05-27) · Original Article ↗

Reilly Opelka has a very unusual step up style with his back foot when he serves.

This video shows the beginning of the evolution of this style but lately he has been stepping up even more behind his front foot.

Very unique and interesting way to pinpoint and load.

Most players will step up and bring their feet close together.

With this style, Opelka gets a very powerful load position and keep his hip back longer than other pinpoint stance servers.

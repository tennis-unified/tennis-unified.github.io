Wisdom from Legendary Spanish Tennis Coach Toni Nadal—No Excuses!

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2019-01-21) · Original Article ↗

“Look for solutions that depend on you and don’t waste energy on blaming external elements that just create a misconception that everything in failure is determining and that it is never our fault.

In this aspect, it’s is advisable not to allow the player to complain not put excuses but to think together what was the mistake, why he made it and how we can solve it with the tools we have.”

–Toni Nadal

Toni Nadal teaches at the Rafa Nadal Academy in Mallorca.

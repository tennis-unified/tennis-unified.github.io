Patology # 82 The Wisdom of Legendary Spanish Tennis Coach William Pato Alvarez

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2019-01-05) · Original Article ↗

“For the returner, when his or her opponent comes to net, his or her first priority is for the passing shot to cross the net making the opponent hit another volley. Only the greatest champions can play risky shots constantly and get success. Normally it’s better to hit passing shots changing direction: if the opponent comes to net with a backhand, you should hit it to his or her forehand volley and vice versa.”

William Pato Alvarez

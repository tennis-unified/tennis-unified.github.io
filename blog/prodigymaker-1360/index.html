Wisdom of Luis Bruguera: The Intuitive Intelligence

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2019-05-29) · Original Article ↗

“A key part of Luis Bruguera’s development system is what he calls intuitive intelligence, which he believes must be developed in all his players for them to achieve maximum on-court performance. The intuitive intelligence, for Luis, must be unlocked by nurturing a player’s natural instincts and developing the subconscious, automatic processes of a player so that, in the heat of battle, he or she can perform instinctively, rather than mechanically or through conscious application. It is for this reason that Bruguera favors the guided discovery and implicit method of teaching. He believes this style of teaching helps develop intuitive intelligence better than any other pedagogical method…”

An excerpt from the bestselling book, The Secrets of Spanish Tennis, by Chris Lewit.

Wisdom of Toni Nadal: Characteristics of a Great Coach

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2020-05-04) · Original Article ↗

“There are characteristics that I always look for in a coach:

1. Kindness

It’s hard to work with someone who is not kind—in many fields—not just tennis.

2. Empathy

Closeness is not incompatible with discipline. It’s fundamental that a kid feels the warmth of a coach. It’s determinant.

3. Body Language

A passive coach who stands by the fence or sits in a chair—players feel that. That coach won’t motivate the player.”

—Toni Nadal

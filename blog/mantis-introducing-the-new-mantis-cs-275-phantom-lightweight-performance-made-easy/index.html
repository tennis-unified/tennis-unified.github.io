Lightweight Tennis Racket - Mantis CS 275 'Phantom' for Easy Play

By Mantis Sport Technical Team · Technical & Gear Guides · Original Article ↗

Not every tennis player wants a heavy racket.

For many players, the biggest improvement comes from choosing a racket that's easier to swing, more forgiving and gives them the confidence to play their best tennis.

That's exactly why we've created the new Mantis CS 275 'Phantom'.

At just 275g and priced at only £115, it's the lightest adult racket in the Mantis range and has been designed specifically for players who want an easier, more enjoyable playing experience without compromising on quality.

Lightweight tennis racket. Comfortable. Confidence Inspiring.

A lighter racket isn't about taking shortcuts—it's about making the game more enjoyable.

The Mantis CS 275 allows you to generate racket head speed more easily, react quicker at the net and play for longer with less fatigue. Whether you're hitting from the baseline or working on your serve, the lightweight construction helps you move the racket naturally and confidently.

Combined with its forgiving 100 sq in head, the result is a racket that offers excellent power, comfort and consistency across the court.

Who is the CS 275 For?

The CS 275 has been developed for a wide range of players, including:

  • Players who are new to tennis and want a racket that's easy to use.
  • Club and intermediate players looking for a lightweight, forgiving frame.
  • Teenagers moving from a junior racket to their first full-size adult racket.
  • Experienced players who simply prefer a lighter, more manoeuvrable racket.
  • Anyone looking for excellent performance without spending hundreds of pounds.

If you enjoy playing regularly and want a racket that helps rather than hinders your game, the CS 275 could be the perfect fit.

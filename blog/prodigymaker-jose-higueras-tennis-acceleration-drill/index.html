Jose Higueras Tennis Acceleration Drill

By Chris Lewit · The Prodigy Maker (2018-11-28) · Original Article ↗

This is is one of Jose’s favorite acceleration drills to develop whip and racquet head speed.

Jose was the tour coach for numerous top pros like Chang, Courier, Federer, and other champions.  He was also the head of coaching for the USTA and has been instrumental in helping to revive American tennis.

I have been fortunate enough to be mentored by Jose over the last few years, visiting him in Palm Springs, shadowing him on court, and calling him with tennis questions.  I’m very grateful for his mentoring and wisdom.  He has made me a better coach.  Thank you Jose!

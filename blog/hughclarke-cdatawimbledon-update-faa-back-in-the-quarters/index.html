Wimbledon Update: FAA Back in the Quarters

Felix Auger Aliassime came through a rollercoaster five-setter against Alejandro Davidovich Fokina 6/7(4) 7/6(6) 6/3 6/7(2) 6/1 to reach his first quarterfinal at Wimbledon since 2021. The serve and forehand have been firing for the Canadian — the usual backbone of his game — and so far he has only been broken once the entire tournament: when serving for the match at 5-3 in the fourth-set yesterday. But I’ve also liked the backhand setup adjustment FAA has made in recent months, lowering the takeback that is better suited to handling faster, lower incoming shots. Compare his Indian Wells set up to the grass season: FAA's backhand setup has yo-yo'd several times throughout his career. I'd prefer the hands closer/the takeback longer, but I think this setup on the right works better for grass' lower bounce and necessarily more aggressive court position, given how much drop Felix gets on his backhand .

Shelton in Montreal

Apologies that this is late ( just like last year’s Canadian recap ) but Thursday finals just aren’t working for me right now. Anyway, it’s always great to see the top players in town. I saw Shelton, Fonseca, Norrie, de Minaur, Fils, Jodar, and Mensik, to name a few. I also got to catch up with Kevin Konfederak (team Norrie), and meet Jim Courier and talk coaching, forehands, extended wrists, etc., which was very cool. left to right: catching up with Kevin Konfederak (team Norrie) ahead of the Norrie vs de Minaur epic, Shelton vs Fonseca in a late afternoon, $27 of sustenance Ben Shelton defended his Canadian Masters title, defeating Brandon Nakashima 6-3 7-6(4) in the final of the Montreal Masters 1000 on Thursday evening. Shelton is now up to 6th in the ATP rankings, and I kind of want to shelve the match somewhat and just talk about Shelton’s game in more detail.

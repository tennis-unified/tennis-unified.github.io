Fault Tolerant Tennis

Total Videos: 151

Fault Tolerant Tennis is a coaching channel focused on the principles of fault tolerance — the ability to recover from off-center contact and unusual body positions. The library covers the backhand, forehand, serve, and overhead, with practical cues for recreational players and competitive juniors alike. Visit faulttoleranttennis.com for more.

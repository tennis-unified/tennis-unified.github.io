ART-863: Endurance Training Methods for Tennis Players

Executive Summary

Tennis endurance training must address the sport's unique energy demands: intermittent high-intensity efforts interspersed with brief recovery periods, lasting 1-5 hours, with the need to recover between matches on consecutive days. Effective endurance training develops three interconnected systems: the aerobic base (for recovery between points and sustained match play), the anaerobic system (for high-intensity rallies), and repeated sprint ability (for the explosive movements that determine point outcomes). A periodized endurance program progresses from aerobic base building in the off-season to tennis-specific conditioning in the pre-season and in-season maintenance.

Energy Systems in Tennis

Aerobic System (60-70% of match energy)

Role: Recovery between points, sustained performance, multi-day tournament play.

Training Methods: - Steady-state running: 30-60 minutes at 65-75% HRmax - Cycling: 45-60 minutes moderate intensity - Swimming: 30-45 minutes continuous

Anaerobic System (20-30% of match energy)

Role: High-intensity rallies, serves, overheads, explosive movements.

Training Methods: - Interval training: 30-90 second efforts at 85-95% HRmax - Tempo runs: 20-30 minutes at lactate threshold - Circuit training: High-intensity circuits with short rest

ATP-PC System (5-10% of match energy)

Role: Explosive first steps, serves, overheads (5-10 second efforts).

Training Methods: - Short sprints: 5-15 seconds at maximum effort - Plyometrics: Box jumps, depth jumps, bounding - Olympic lifts: Hang cleans, push presses

Aerobic Base Building

Off-Season Aerobic Development

Frequency: 3-4 sessions per week Duration: 30-60 minutes per session Intensity: 60-75% of maximum heart rate

Aerobic Training Protocols

Protocol 1: Steady-State Running - Duration: 30-60 minutes - Intensity: 65-75% HRmax (conversational pace) - Frequency: 3 sessions per week - Progression: Increase duration by 5 minutes per week

Protocol 2: Fartlek Training - Duration: 30-45 minutes - Structure: Mix of moderate and fast-paced segments - Example: 5 min easy, 3 min moderate, 2 min fast, repeat - Frequency: 2 sessions per week

Protocol 3: Cross-Training - Cycling: 45-60 minutes moderate intensity - Swimming: 30-45 minutes continuous - Rowing: 30-45 minutes moderate intensity - Frequency: 2-3 sessions per week (can replace running)

Aerobic Benchmarks

Level VO2max 3 km Time Cooper Test (12 min)
Recreational 35-45 mL/kg/min 14-18 min 2200-2600 m
Competitive Junior 45-55 mL/kg/min 11-14 min 2600-3000 m
Professional 50-65 mL/kg/min 9-12 min 3000-3400 m

Anaerobic Capacity Development

Pre-Season Anaerobic Training

Frequency: 2-3 sessions per week Intensity: 85-95% of maximum heart rate

Anaerobic Training Protocols

Protocol 1: Interval Running - Duration: 30-90 seconds per interval - Rest: Equal to work duration - Sets: 6-10 intervals - Example: 8 x 400m at 5K pace with 90-second rest

Protocol 2: Tempo Runs - Duration: 20-30 minutes continuous - Intensity: Lactate threshold (comfortably hard) - Frequency: 2 sessions per week

Protocol 3: Circuit Training - Duration: 30-45 minutes - Structure: 8-10 exercises, 45 seconds work, 15 seconds rest - Frequency: 2 sessions per week

Anaerobic Benchmarks

Level 400m Time Blood Lactate (post-match)
Recreational 80-100 sec 3-5 mmol
Competitive Junior 65-80 sec 5-8 mmol
Professional 55-70 sec 6-12 mmol

Repeated Sprint Ability (RSA)

RSA is the ability to perform repeated sprints with minimal performance decrement—critical for tennis where 3-5 explosive movements occur per point with only 20-30 seconds of rest.

RSA Training Protocols

Protocol 1: Short Sprints - 10 x 20-meter sprints - 20-second rest between sprints - Focus: First-step quickness and acceleration

Protocol 2: Tennis-Specific Sprints - 10 x baseline-to-net sprints (10-15 meters) - 30-second rest between sprints - Serve shadow after each sprint - Focus: Serve-and-volley transition

Protocol 3: Court Coverage - 6 x court-width lateral sprints - 30-second rest between sprints - Focus: Lateral movement speed

Protocol 4: Rally Simulation - 4 x 45-second high-intensity on-court drills - 90-second rest between drills - Focus: Sustained high-intensity output

RSA Benchmarks

Level 10 x 20m RSA (total time) Decrement
Recreational >40 sec >15%
Competitive Junior 35-40 sec 10-15%
Professional <35 sec <10%

On-Court Endurance Drills

The Suicide Drill

  • Sprint from baseline to service line and back
  • Sprint to net and back
  • Sprint to opposite service line and back
  • Repeat 3-5 times with 60-second rest between sets
  • Focus: Court coverage speed and repeated sprint ability

The 10-Ball Drill

  • Coach feeds 10 balls rapidly to alternating sides
  • Player must return each ball to a target
  • 30-second rest, repeat 4-6 sets
  • Focus: High-intensity rallying with incomplete recovery

The Shadow Rally

  • Player executes full movement patterns (split-step, move, stroke, recovery)
  • Continuous for 45-60 seconds
  • 60-second rest, repeat 4-6 sets
  • Focus: Match-like movement intensity

The Serve-and-Recover Drill

  • Player serves and transitions to the net
  • Coach feeds a passing shot
  • Player volleys, recovers to baseline, serves again
  • Continuous for 10 serves
  • Focus: Serve-and-volley conditioning

Endurance Training Program

Off-Season (Weeks 1-6): Aerobic Base Building

Week 1-2: - 3 x steady-state running (30 min at 65% HRmax) - 2 x cycling (45 min moderate) - 1 x swimming (30 min continuous)

Week 3-4: - 3 x steady-state running (40 min at 70% HRmax) - 2 x fartlek training (30 min) - 1 x swimming (35 min continuous)

Week 5-6: - 3 x steady-state running (45 min at 70% HRmax) - 2 x fartlek training (35 min) - 2 x RSA training (short sprints)

Pre-Season (Weeks 7-10): Anaerobic Development

Week 7-8: - 2 x interval running (8 x 400m at 5K pace) - 2 x tempo runs (20 min) - 2 x RSA training - 2 x on-court endurance drills

Week 9-10: - 2 x interval running (10 x 400m at 5K pace) - 2 x tempo runs (25 min) - 2 x RSA training - 3 x on-court endurance drills

In-Season: Maintenance

Tournament Weeks: - 1 x RSA training (if time allows) - 1 x on-court endurance drill (if time allows) - Focus on recovery between matches

Training Weeks: - 2 x interval training or tempo runs - 2 x RSA training - 2 x on-court endurance drills

Practical Application: "The Endurance Assessment Battery"

Assess endurance with three tests:

  1. Aerobic Fitness: Cooper Test (12-minute run) — target >3000m for professionals
  2. Anaerobic Capacity: 400m sprint — target <70 seconds for professionals
  3. Repeated Sprint Ability: 10 x 20m sprints with 20-second rest — target <35 seconds total

Retest every 8-12 weeks to track improvement.

Diagnostic & Training Matrix

Observation Failure Result Corrective Strategy
Fatigue in third sets Insufficient aerobic base Performance decline in extended matches Aerobic base building; steady-state running; cross-training
Cannot maintain intensity in long rallies Insufficient anaerobic capacity Lost extended points Interval training; tempo runs; circuit training
Slow recovery between points Poor aerobic fitness, insufficient recovery training Cumulative fatigue RSA training; on-court endurance drills; recovery training
Performance decline in consecutive-day matches Insufficient match fitness, poor recovery Reduced performance in later matches Match simulation training; recovery protocols; load management

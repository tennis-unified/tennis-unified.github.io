Advanced Deep Dives¶

Focused exploration of specific advanced topics. The biomechanics, the felt sense, the body-mechanics behind every stroke.

The Deep Dives¶

  • Embodied Cognition — how the body knows what it's doing, and why "thinking about it" often makes it worse
  • Fascia Spiral — the continuous connective tissue web that transmits force from foot to racket
  • Head Position and Vestibular — balance, gaze stability, the role of the inner ear in tennis
  • Proprioception — the felt sense of where your body is in space
  • Reflex Arcs — the fast neural loops that handle incoming balls
  • Tensegrity Body — the body as a tensegrity structure, and what that means for force transfer
  • Two Engines — the upper-body and lower-body coordination that produces the L-angle
  • X-Factor Anatomy — the hip-shoulder separation that generates power

How to Use This Section¶

The deep-dives are independent. Pick the one that matches the question in your body right now.

Read once, then take it to the court. The article describes the mechanics. Your body has to learn them.

Then read it again, a month later, after practice. The article hasn't changed. Your eyes have.

Volleying¶

The volley is hit before the ball bounces. It requires quick reflexes, soft hands, and good positioning at the net.

Grip¶

Continental grip for all volleys: - Allows forehand and backhand without changing grip - Natural racket face control - Best for quick reactions

Ready Position at Net¶

  • Feet shoulder-width, knees bent
  • Weight on balls of feet
  • Racket up at chest height, out front
  • Eyes on opponent's contact point
  • Split step as opponent hits

Forehand Volley¶

Technique¶

  1. Turn shoulders slightly (unit turn)
  2. Racket back minimal - just to side
  3. Step forward with opposite foot
  4. Punch/block - firm wrist, short motion
  5. Contact in front, racket face slightly open
  6. Follow-through minimal - finish at target

Key Points¶

  • Firm wrist - don't swing, block
  • Contact in front - see ball hit strings
  • Racket face open - controls height
  • Step into it - weight transfer

Backhand Volley¶

Technique¶

  1. Turn shoulders more than forehand
  2. Racket back to backhand side
  3. Step forward with front foot
  4. Block/punch - non-dominant hand releases
  5. Contact in front, slightly to side
  6. Finish racket at target

Key Points¶

  • Non-dominant hand guides racket back
  • Elbow in - close to body
  • Firm wrist - no break
  • Shoulder turn provides power

Volley Types¶

Volley Situation Technique
Punch Standard, mid-court Firm, short, drive
Block Fast incoming Absorb pace, redirect
Drop Touch, short angle Soft hands, finesse
Swinging High, slow ball Longer swing, put away
Half-volley Ball at feet Short hop, lift

Common Errors¶

Error Result Fix
Swinging Long, no control Block/punch, firm wrist
Racket drops Net errors Keep racket up
Late contact Pop-ups, errors Meet ball further front
No split step Slow reaction Split step every time
Wrong grip Can't control face Continental always

Positioning¶

Position Distance from Net Purpose
Aggressive 3-4 feet Put away, finish points
Neutral 6-8 feet React to passes/lobs
Defensive 10+ feet Cover lobs, buy time

Practice Drills¶

  1. Wall volleys - rapid fire, control
  2. Partner feed - controlled practice
  3. Volley-volley - cooperative, consistency
  4. Approach + volley - game situation
  5. Reflex drill - close range, quick hands

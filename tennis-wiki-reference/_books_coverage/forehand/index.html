Forehand — The Kinetic Chain

The kinetic chain behind the forehand, straight-arm vs. double-bend geometry, stance selection, and the tactical case for the run-around forehand.

The modern forehand is built ground-up: ground reaction force from the legs through hip rotation, shoulder rotation, and arm whip, with racket-head speed peaking at contact. Stance selection (open, neutral, closed) and geometry (straight-arm vs. double-bend) trade off power, control, and injury risk.

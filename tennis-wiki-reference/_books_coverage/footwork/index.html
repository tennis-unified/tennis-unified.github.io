Footwork & Movement — The Split-Step and Foot Anchors

The split-step's mechanics and neurology, and the specific foot anchor behind every stroke from net to baseline.

The split-step is a small plyometric hop that pre-loads the calves and primes the nervous system to react. Each stroke has a specific foot anchor (the back foot in a forehand, the lead foot in a volley, the pivot foot on a serve) that determines the kinetic chain's geometry.

Backhand — Non-Dominant Arm & Geometry

Starting with the non-dominant arm's real role in the two-handed backhand.

The two-handed backhand's dominant hand drives the swing; the non-dominant hand sets the racket's path, controls depth, and provides about 30-50% of the kinetic energy depending on stance. The one-handed backhand trades power for reach and slice variety.

Serve — Mechanics, Perception, and Tactics

The mechanical, perceptual, and tactical model behind serving and returning: leg-driven power, the eight-stage diagnostic, and reading a serve before it's struck.

The modern serve is leg-driven, kinetic-chain sequenced, and tactically loaded — the eight-stage diagnostic isolates which link is breaking (toss, trophy, leg drive, hip rotation, shoulder rotation, contact, pronation, follow-through). Returners read the toss and the server's body shape to anticipate direction and spin.

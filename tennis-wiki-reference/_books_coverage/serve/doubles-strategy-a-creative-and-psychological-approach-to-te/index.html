The Serve The Standard Serves Your service is most effective if you vary the speed and spin of each serve and thereby keep the receiver off balance. To accom- plish such differences in delivery, change your racquet grip and your ball toss, not the speed of your stroke. Your racquet grip and ball toss determine the angle of your racquet face when it hits the ball, and it is the angle of your racquet face that determines how much of the power of your stroke will be distributed to velocity of the ball and how much to spin of the ball. There are three standard serves—flat, slice, and twist. A fourth serve—the American twist—is a variation of the twist. ea]

Grip — Bevels, Pressure, and the Grip Pulse

Grip pressure, timing, anchoring, and the bevel system, down to the millisecond-level mechanics of the grip pulse.

Modern grip theory covers the eight bevels, pressure gradients during the swing, the millisecond timing of the grip pulse (relaxed at preparation, firmest at contact, relaxing again through release), and the anchoring cues that keep the handle consistent under stress.

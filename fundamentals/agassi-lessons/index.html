Agassi Lessons

Andre Agassi

This section hosts the Andre Agassi Lessons video library — a curated collection of drills, observations, and mental cues from one of the most complete players in tennis history. Andre Agassi is an eight-time Grand Slam singles champion, an Olympic gold medalist, a career Golden Slam winner, and was inducted into the International Tennis Hall of Fame in 2011. These lessons capture the small adjustments and big-picture thinking that defined his game — and the lessons he wants to share with the next generation of players.

Andre Agassi played professional tennis for two decades (1986–2006) and was inducted into the International Tennis Hall of Fame in 2011. After retiring, he has stayed involved in the game as a coach and mentor. These videos are part of his ongoing effort to teach the lessons he learned at the top of the game to players of all levels.
